# Research basis

The tool follows the local research-workflow principle in `ACADEMIC_RESEARCH_DEEP_DIVE.md`: milestones and supervision records are transparent, portable, and human-reviewed. It is not a thesis-format or institutional-policy validator.

- EQUATOR Network — <https://www.equator-network.org/>
- Center for Open Science TOP Guidelines — <https://www.cos.io/initiatives/top-guidelines>
- UKRI data management planning resources — <https://www.ukri.org/plan-your-project/research-data-management/>
- Digital Curation Centre DMP resources — <https://www.dcc.ac.uk/resources/data-management-plans>

These sources inform provenance and research planning principles. They do not establish thesis dates, institutional approval rules, or supervisor obligations. User-entered dates and statuses are organizational records, not evidence of task quality.
